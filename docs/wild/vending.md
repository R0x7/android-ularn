# Vending machines, junk food, the rubber duck and the Roomba

Someone put vending machines in the dungeon: about 40% of levels 1–10 and V1–V3
have one, and there is always one next to the DnD store on the home level.
Step on it (or press the **Vending** context button) to **buy** junk food for
50 gold or **kick** it. A kick may shake something loose, or make the machine
wobble ominously. Kick a wobbling machine again and it falls on you (10–25
damage, "crushed by a vending machine").

Eat junk food with **e**, or when you find it on the floor. **Fizz** hastes you
for 30 turns. **Pizza** heals a quarter of your max HP and sometimes gives +1 CON.
An **energy drink** gives back 8 spell points but leaves you confused for a few
turns. The **rubber duck** is "read" with **r**, or rubbed when you find it. It
identifies every potion and scroll you carry, three times per game.

From level 3 down, a **Roomba** sometimes trundles around eating anything left
on the floor. Kill it and it coughs everything back up.

Turn it all off with Settings → Wild features → *Vending machines, junk food, the rubber duck and the
Roomba* (`WILDF_VENDING`).
