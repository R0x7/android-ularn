# Fishing and the Fountain Kraken

Fountains have fish in them. You need a **fishing rod**: the DnD store sells
one for 250 gold, and one is lying somewhere on dungeon level 1 or 2. With a rod
in your pack, stepping on a fountain (or the **Fountain** context button) adds
**(f) fish** to the drink / wash prompt.

A cast takes 1–5 turns. Monsters keep moving, and if one hits you, you drop your
line. You may reel in a **fish** (eat it with **e**: heals 10 + your level), nothing,
an **old boot** (the trading post pays 1 gold for it; one in five has a small gem
inside the first time you pick it up), a **message in a bottle** (read it with
**r** for a fortune; then the bottle is gone), a ring, or a potion someone threw in.
Every catch makes you a better angler, so you get fewer empty hooks and boots.
After a catch the fountain may dry up.

Now and then the fountain turns out to be deeper than it looked. The
**fountain kraken** surfaces where the fountain was and throws you aside. It is
attached to the plumbing and can't get more than 2 squares from its old fountain,
so you can run away. If you kill it, it drops a pile of gold.

Turn it all off with Settings → Wild features → *Fishing at fountains*
(`WILDF_FISHING`).
