# Dungeon Radio

The dungeon has a soundtrack: NES-style chiptune music and square-wave sound
effects, synthesized on the phone as they play (no audio files). The track
follows you: *Town* on the surface, *Shallows* on levels 1-4, *Depths* below,
*Volcano* in the volcano, *Shop* inside the stores, and a *Game Over* or
*Victory* stinger at the end. Kills, hits, pickups, gold, spells, stairs and
level-ups each have their own blip.

Three **cassette tapes** (the little tape on the floor) are hidden on dungeon
levels 2 and 6 and volcano level 2. Step on one to unlock a bonus track:
*Elevator Music* (plays in the bank), *Surf's Up* (plays while fishing) and
*Boss Fight* (plays when a demon lord is in sight). Tapes stay unlocked for
every later game.

In a game, **Menu → Radio** shows what's playing, lets you skip to the next
track or pick a tape, and has the music and sound-effect volume sliders.
The radio goes quiet when the app is in the background.

Turn it off with **Settings → Wild features → Dungeon Radio** (new games
then also have no tapes). The old "No beeping" option only silences the
game's own beep.
