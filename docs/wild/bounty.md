# Bounty board

The town crier keeps one WANTED poster up: a monster from about one level
deeper than you have been so far, and a gold reward that grows with that
depth (125 gold for a level-1 monster, 3,700 for the deepest kind).

* **Use:** the poster is announced whenever you are in town. Kill one of the
  wanted monsters anywhere and the game tells you the bounty is yours; the
  gold is paid the next time you are back in town, where a new poster goes up.
* **Stuck?** A poster nobody answers is taken down after about 2,500 turns (or
  at once if you genocided the species) and replaced with a fresh one.
* **See it:** the end of the `\` (known spells / scrolls / potions) list.
* **Disable:** Settings → Wild features → "Town bounty board"
  (`WILDF_BOUNTY`). The choice is stored with each character when it is
  created.
