# Hats

Five ridiculous hats, drawn on top of your character while worn.

* **Where:** any dungeon or volcano level may have one lying around (about a
  quarter of the levels near the top, more often deeper down), and the DND
  store sells all five on its last page.
* **Use:** `W` puts a hat on (one at a time; it shows as "(on your head)" in
  the inventory). `T` takes it off; with armour or a shield on as well, `T`
  asks whether the hat or the armour comes off. Dropping, selling or losing
  the hat takes it off too.
* **Effects:** traffic cone +2 AC and 10% of melee blows swerve past you;
  propeller beanie flies you over pits and trap doors; wizard hat doubles
  spell regeneration; party hat +3 CHA and 10% off at the DND store; tinfoil
  hat makes you immune to confusion.
* **Disable:** Settings → Wild features → Hats (`WILDF_HATS`). The choice is
  stored with each character when it is created.
