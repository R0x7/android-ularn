# LarnCoin and the Tax Auditor

The First National Bank of Ularn now trades **LarnCoin**, a very volatile
cryptocurrency. It starts at 100 gold. Every 10 turns the price drifts a few
percent, and it tends to keep going the way it last went. Now and then there is
news that moves it 30–60% in one go ("A famous gnome posts about LarnCoin.",
"A whale dumps."). Above 1500 gold the founder may leave for a volcano, and
then the price drops to 1.

In the bank, **b** buys coins (paid from your account first, then from your
pocket) and **c** cashes them out into your account. The **LarnCoin ATM** next
to the bank on the home level (step on it, or press the **ATM** context button)
buys and sells for cash at a 5% fee. It still works while the bank is frozen
over unpaid taxes. Coins count towards your final score at the price on the
day. A small ticker (Ⓛ 123 ▲) appears at the right end of the HUD while you
hold coins, or on a turn when the price moves.

If you owe the LRS taxes, a **tax auditor** may turn up by the dungeon entrance
while you are on the home level. Each time it gets you, it garnishes 10% of the
gold you carry and puts it towards the tax bill. Pay your taxes and it leaves.
You can also kill it.

To turn it all off, go to Settings → Wild features → *LarnCoin at the bank (and the
Tax Auditor)* (`WILDF_COIN`).
