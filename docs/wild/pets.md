# Pets

A mysterious egg that hatches into a loyal little monster, if you carry it long enough.

* **Where:** one speckled, faintly glowing egg lies somewhere on each of dungeon
  levels 2–5.
* **Hatching:** pick it up and carry it. After 300 turns in your pack it wobbles,
  taps, then cracks open next to you. Out comes a jackal, a snake, a giant ant or a
  floating eye, or once in a while a hell hound. The egg only counts down while you
  carry it and while you have no pet.
* **Your pet** has a small red heart drawn on it. It follows you around the level.
  It bites monsters next to it or close to you, and they can bite back. It heals
  slowly. If you walk into it, you swap places with it instead of hitting it.
  Feeding it a fish (`e` with the pet next to you) heals it 10 HP.
* **Stairs:** a pet next to you when you change level comes along. Otherwise it
  stays on that level ("You hear a sad whine behind you.") and is still there when
  you come back. Saving keeps it.
* **Losing it:** it can die in a fight. Your own spells can hurt it too. If it is
  teleported, polymorphed or genocided, it's gone. Then the next egg you carry can
  hatch.
* **Disable:** Settings → Wild features → "Mysterious eggs hatch into pets"
  (`WILDF_PETS`). The choice is stored with each character when it is created.
